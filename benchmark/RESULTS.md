# Benchmark results

Generated by `python tools/benchmark.py baseline`; do not edit by hand. How the corpus was chosen and labelled, and how to reproduce these numbers: [README.md](README.md).

- Corpus: 53 repositories pinned to exact commits ([corpus.json](corpus.json)), 12 of them held out.
- Tuned split: 1320 labelled findings, 1178 true and 142 false (89.2%). Rules were fixed while looking at these findings, so this number flatters them.
- Held-out split: 271 labelled findings, 217 true and 54 false (80.1%). No rule is changed because of these findings; this is the number that decides whether a rule may leave preview.
- Stable rules only (what `--fail-on` acts on): tuned 94.8% (706/745), held-out 93.8% (122/130). Most false positives are in preview rules.
- Without the 8 deliberately vulnerable training apps: 1312 findings, 1150 true and 162 false (87.7%).
- Unlabelled findings: 0.
- A precision needs at least 5 labelled findings; below that the table says `not enough data`.
- Seeded recall counts the `expect:` cases in tests/fixtures and benchmark/seeded that each rule reports.
