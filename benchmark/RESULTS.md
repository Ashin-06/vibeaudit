# Benchmark results

Generated by `python tools/benchmark.py baseline`; do not edit by hand. How the corpus was chosen and labelled, and how to reproduce these numbers: [README.md](README.md).

- Corpus: 40 repositories pinned to exact commits ([corpus.json](corpus.json)), 8 of them held out.
- Tuned split: 570 labelled findings, 506 true and 64 false (88.8%). Rules were fixed while looking at these findings, so this number flatters them.
- Held-out split: 146 labelled findings, 131 true and 15 false (89.7%). No rule is changed because of these findings; this is the number that decides whether a rule may leave preview.
- Unlabelled findings: 0.
- A precision needs at least 5 labelled findings; below that the table says `not enough data`.
- Seeded recall counts the `expect:` cases in tests/fixtures and benchmark/seeded that each rule reports.
