# Benchmark results

Generated by `python tools/benchmark.py baseline`; do not edit by hand. How the corpus was chosen and labelled, and how to reproduce these numbers: [README.md](README.md).

- Corpus: 141 repositories pinned to exact commits ([corpus.json](corpus.json)), 46 of them held out.
- Tuned split: 3264 labelled findings, 2532 true and 732 false (77.6%). Rules were fixed while looking at these findings, so this number flatters them.
- Held-out split: 1066 labelled findings, 666 true and 400 false (62.5%). Rules are not tuned on these findings, with one exception: the causes of the AUTH005, DATA006, SEC001 and SEC004 false positives on the 8 repositories held out when the corpus grew to 125 were fixed in general form, so this number is optimistic for those four rules, which stay in preview until a fresh held-out batch measures them. This is the number that decides whether a rule may leave preview.
- Stable findings only (what `--fail-on` acts on; a stable rule's findings in a preview language count as preview): tuned 93.2% (1251/1342), held-out 93.6% (426/455). Most false positives are in preview rules.
- Without the 15 deliberately vulnerable training apps: 3882 findings, 2813 true and 1069 false (72.5%).
- Unlabelled findings: 0.
- A precision needs at least 5 labelled findings; below that the table says `not enough data`.
- Seeded recall counts the `expect:` cases in tests/fixtures and benchmark/seeded that each rule reports.
